Why I do this

I grew up in Goodnestone and have lived around Canterbury most of my life. A few years ago my uncle ran a clay shooting business near Dover. He was great with a shotgun and great with his customers, but he wanted nothing to do with computers or websites. So I put him online, generated the orders for him, and he got on with what he loved. I've spent 25 years in IT, and I still think providing no-fuss online support is the most useful thing I can do for a local business.

I'm not an agency and there's no call centre. When you ring, you get me.

Common questions

I'm not good with computers. Is that a problem?

Not at all. That's who I'm here for. I'll do the technical parts and show you only what you need, at your pace.

Do I have to pay every month?

No. Each job is a one-off price. The yearly check-up is optional, and you can stop it whenever you like.

Who owns the website?

You do. The website, the web address and all the logins are in your name. If you ever want someone else to look after it, you can take it with you.

What if I don't like what you've made?

You see everything before it goes live, and changes are part of the job. You pay when you're happy.

Is AI safe for my business?

It can be, if it's set up carefully. I make sure your customers' details stay private and that nothing goes out without you checking it.
