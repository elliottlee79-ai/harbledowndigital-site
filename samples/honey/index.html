Why it tastes different

Supermarket honey is blended from many countries and heated so it stays runny. Ours is spun from the frame, strained once and jarred, so it keeps the flavour of whatever was flowering that month.

Spring honey is pale and floral from orchard blossom. By August the bees are on bramble and clover, and the honey turns darker and richer.
